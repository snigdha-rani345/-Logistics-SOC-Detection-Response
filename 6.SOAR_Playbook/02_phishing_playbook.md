# SOAR Playbook 2: Phishing Email Response

## Step 1: Triage
- How many users got this email?
- Did anyone click the link?

## Step 2: Contain
- BLOCK the sender domain
- DELETE the email from all inboxes

## Step 3: Investigate
- What was the malicious link?
- Any credentials stolen?

## Step 4: Recover
- RESET password for clicked users
- Enable MFA
- Send awareness mail to team
